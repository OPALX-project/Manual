=== The BINNING Command

opal-begin
In _OPAL_ the energy bin definition is done at the DISTRIBUTION. 
opal-end

opalx-begin
In _OPALX_ the energy bin defined as a process (command) and the given to the fieldsolver
as an argument.

----
BINS1: BINNING, 
    MAXBINS      = 120, 
    DESIREDWIDTH = 0.2, 
    BINNINGALPHA = 1.1, 
    BINNINGBETA  = 1.6, 
    PARAMETER    = VELOCITYZ, // Could be "VELOCITYZ", "POSITIONZ", "PZ" or "GAMMAZ"
    DUMPBINSFILE = "data/bin-dump.json",
    DUMPBINSFREQ = 1; 
----
